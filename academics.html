        Research & Innovation

        APCian Research Forum

        Creating awareness and opportunities in research & development — from funded projects to doctoral scholarship.

        A Culture of Inquiry, Guided by the ARF

        The APCian Research Forum is formed with one representative faculty from each discipline, headed by the Research Coordinator. Its objective: to create awareness and opportunities in research and development among faculty & research scholars, and to inculcate the interest to take up research projects from national funding agencies.
