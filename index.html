      Since 1974 · Thoothukudi

      A Legacy of Lighting Lamps, Not Just Degrees

      Founded by the visionary philanthropist Kulapathi Sri A.P.C. Veerabahu, A.P.C. Mahalaxmi College for Women was born of a single belief — that educating a girl uplifts an entire community. For over five decades, the college has carried first-generation learners from the coastal villages of Thoothukudi into classrooms, laboratories and leadership.

      Affiliated to Manonmaniam Sundaranar University, the institution blends academic rigour with the timeless values of Tamil culture — character, discipline and courage.

      • NAAC-accredited & NIRF-ranked institution
      • Aided & self-financed UG, PG & research streams
      • Deep focus on rural & first-gen learners
      • Vibrant NSS, YRC, sports & fine-arts culture

      • To cultivate critical thinking for lifelong learning

        Since 1974

        Five Decades of Lighting Lamps

        From one building and a dream — to a thriving hub of women's higher education. Walk the journey.

        1974

        The Founding

        Kulapathi Sri A.P.C. Veerabahu establishes the college, opening the doors of higher education to the daughters of Thoothukudi's villages.

        1979

        Science Awakens

        The Chemistry department and its laboratories begin — the campus' first step into experimental science.

        1985

        Commerce Wing

        B.Com. is introduced, preparing young women for banking, accounting and business careers.

        1994

        The Computer Era

        Computer Science arrives with a brand-new computing centre — APCians enter the digital age early.

        2004

        PG Expansion

        M.Sc., M.Com. and M.A. programmes launch, letting students complete their masters in their hometown.

        2014

        Research Recognised

        Departments gain recognition for M.Phil. and Ph.D. research — scholars now guide scholars.

        2020

        Digital Campus

        A full ERP transformation: online admissions, e-attendance, digital fee payments and portal logins for all.

        2024

        Golden Jubilee & NIRF

        Fifty golden years celebrated — alongside a prestigious national listing in the NIRF college rankings.

        2026

        The Next Chapter

        Smart classrooms, grander cultural fests like APCian CARNIVAL 2K26, and a campus that keeps growing — with you.
