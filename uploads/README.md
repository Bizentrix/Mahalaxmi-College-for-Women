## Research basis
The official APCM site, 2025 brochure, infrastructure/SSR material, department pages, IQAC, APCM Academy, career guidance, alumnae and student-support pages were checked.

The official infrastructure material lists multiple blocks, laboratories, smart classes, ICT hall, library, auditorium, sports facilities and herbal garden.
The brochure lists aided/unaided UG, PG, research and certificate/short-term programmes.
Department pages contain faculty, milestones, publications, activities and resource sections.

## Production rule
Use real APCM media wherever possible.
Get the following from the college before final deployment:
- original SVG/PNG logo
- principal and secretary portraits
- original campus photographs
- original laboratory/library/classroom photographs
- event photos
- licensed original MP4s
- current admissions data
- current contact/address data
- official accreditation logos and documents
